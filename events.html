IIEE - Council of Student Chapters Programs and Events

The Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. – Council of Student Chapters (IIEE-CSC) organizes a series of academic, leadership, professional development, and community-oriented programs throughout the year. These activities provide Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students with opportunities to expand their knowledge, develop technical and leadership competencies, build meaningful connections, and actively participate in the IIEE community.

Regional Student Congress Upcoming

September 19, 2026 (Mega Webinar)

September 26, 2026 (EE Tutorial)

October 3, 2026 (EET Tutorial)

October 22-24, 2026 (RSCon Event Proper)

▼

Event Details

Date: September 19, 2026; September 26, 2026; October 3, 2026; October 22-24, 2026

Venue: Respective Host Schools of 10 Regions

Description

The Regional Student Congress (RSCon) is a regional gathering of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students featuring academic competitions, professional development activities, programs, and opportunities for collaboration. It provides students with an avenue to exchange knowledge, demonstrate their capabilities, and strengthen camaraderie across the region.

Mega Webinar and RCSC Seminar

A learning and professional development activity designed to enhance students' knowledge in Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology. The seminar provides discussions on relevant technical and non-technical topics while giving participants opportunities to learn from professionals and industry practitioners.

EE Tutorial

The EE Tutorial serves as a preparation program for participants of the Regional Math Wizard and Regional Quiz Show. Designed for aspiring quizzers, the tutorial strengthens subject knowledge, sharpens problem-solving skills, and introduces effective strategies in preparation for the flagship academic competitions of the Regional Student Congress.

RCSC Election

The RCSC Election provides qualified voting members with the opportunity to elect the next set of officers of the Regional Council of Student Chapters. Pursuant to the 2007 Implementing Rules and Regulations, the election is conducted through secret balloting during the three-day Regional Student Congress, with the specific election date determined by the RCSC.

Regional Math Wizard

The Regional Math Wizard (RMW) is a premier academic competition that challenges students in arithmetic, mathematics, and applied mathematical concepts. It brings together participants from different parts of the region and serves as the regional qualifying competition for the National Math Wizard.

Regional Quiz Show

The Regional Quiz Show (RQS) is a prestigious academic competition focusing on Electrical Engineering-related subjects. It provides students with an avenue to demonstrate their technical knowledge, analytical thinking, and problem-solving abilities while determining the regional representatives for the National Quiz Show.

Objectives

The Regional Student Congress aims to:

  1. Promote excellence in Electrical Engineering education through the 25th Regional Math Wizard and 39th Regional Quiz Show.
  2. Strengthen camaraderie and encourage the exchange of knowledge and ideas among students across the region.
  3. Provide opportunities for students to share innovative ideas, experiences, and perspectives in Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology.
  4. Facilitate the election of the next set of RCSC officers who will represent and serve the region.

National Student Convention

November 24-26, 2026 (SMX Convention Center Manila)

▼

Event Details

Date: November 24-26, 2026

Venue: SMX Convention Center Manila

Description

The National Student Convention (NSCon) is a nationwide gathering of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students that brings together academic competitions, technical activities, professional development programs, social events, and recognition programs. It provides a national platform for students to demonstrate their knowledge, skills, creativity, leadership, and sportsmanship.

Mega Webinar

A virtual learning event featuring technical and non-technical discussions designed to help students expand their knowledge and gain insights relevant to their future careers. It also provides an opportunity for students and professionals to interact in preparation for the National Student Convention.

EEsports: MLBB, CODM & VALORANT

An open esports competition featuring Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, Call of Duty: Mobile, and VALORANT. Participants from across the country compete through teamwork, strategy, communication, and competitive gaming skills.

National Math Wizard

The National Math Wizard (NMW) brings together the winners of the regional competitions from across the country for a national-level competition in mathematics and applied mathematical concepts. It challenges participants to demonstrate speed, accuracy, analytical thinking, and problem-solving ability.

National Quiz Show

The National Quiz Show (NQS) is a national academic competition focusing on various Electrical Engineering subjects. Regional winners converge to demonstrate their technical knowledge, analytical abilities, and mastery of concepts in the field.

Clash of Intellects in Engineering Sciences and Allied Subjects (CIENSCIA)

A challenging academic competition covering science-related subjects within the field of engineering. CIENSCIA provides students with an avenue to demonstrate their breadth of knowledge, critical thinking, and analytical skills.

Philippine Electrical Code Quiz Show

The Philippine Electrical Code Quiz Show (PECQS) assesses students' understanding and application of the Philippine Electrical Code (PEC). The competition encourages participants to develop familiarity with electrical standards and their proper application in electrical installations and practices.

Electrical Technology Quiz Show

The Electrical Technology Quiz Show (ETQS) is a national academic competition dedicated to Electrical Technology students. It provides a platform for participants to showcase their knowledge and understanding of subjects and specializations relevant to the Electrical Technology profession.

STEMEEFY Quiz Show

A national quiz competition for Senior High School students that tests knowledge in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). The activity promotes academic excellence, critical thinking, and healthy competition while introducing students to fields related to engineering and technology.

Silakbo Fun Run

The Silakbo Fun Run promotes physical wellness, camaraderie, and active participation among students and professionals. It provides participants with an opportunity to come together outside academic competitions while fostering unity within the IIEE community.

Dagitab Awards

The Dagitab Awards is a recognition program honoring outstanding Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students and chapters for their achievements, innovation, service, and contributions to the IIEE-CSC community.

Objectives

The National Student Convention aims to:

  1. Bring together Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students from across the country for interaction and exchange of knowledge and ideas.
  2. Provide a national platform for the winners of the Regional Math Wizard and Regional Quiz Show to compete in their respective national competitions.
  3. Promote excellence in Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology through academic, technical, and skills-based competitions.
  4. Familiarize students with developments, opportunities, and career pathways in Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology through technical and non-technical webinars.
  5. Provide Senior High School students with an avenue to demonstrate their knowledge and skills in STEM.
  6. Give participants the opportunity to experience the IIEE Annual National Convention and engage with electrical practitioners and emerging technologies.

Electrical Engineering and Technology Students' Summit

To be Announce

▼

Event Details

Date: To be announced

Venue: To be announced

Description

The Electrical Engineering and Technology Students' Summit is a comprehensive student development program that brings together academic competitions, technical activities, research, skills development, safety awareness, creative design, and recognition. It provides students with opportunities to demonstrate practical competencies while gaining exposure to current industry practices and professional opportunities.

Mega Webinar

A virtual learning event featuring three Electrical Engineering and Technology topics and one non-technical subject, presented by professionals. The activity aims to broaden students' technical knowledge and provide relevant insights for their academic and professional development.

Skills Olympics

The Skills Olympics promotes technical excellence, innovation, teamwork, and practical competency among Electrical Engineering and Technology students. Through hands-on challenges involving areas such as Building Wiring and Motor Controls, participants demonstrate their technical skills while developing confidence and professional discipline.

Basic Occupational Safety and Health Electrical Safety Quiz Show (BOSHEQS)

A competitive academic activity that evaluates students' knowledge and awareness of Basic Occupational Safety and Health (BOSH) principles and electrical safety practices. It promotes a strong culture of safety among future electrical practitioners.

Research Congress

The Research Congress provides a formal platform for students to present and discuss original research, technical innovations, studies, and developments related to Electrical Engineering and Technology. It encourages research culture, critical thinking, and knowledge sharing among students.

EEllumination Design Contest

A technical design competition where students demonstrate their proficiency in developing electrical plans and layouts using AutoCAD and Microsoft Excel. The competition emphasizes accuracy, technical competence, and adherence to applicable industry standards.

Dagitab Awards

The Dagitab Awards recognizes outstanding Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology student chapters for their excellence, innovation, leadership, and meaningful contributions to the IIEE community.

Objectives

The Electrical Engineering and Technology Students' Summit aims to:

  1. Foster camaraderie and physical wellness among Electrical Engineering and Technology students through social and fellowship activities.
  2. Promote a strong culture of occupational and electrical safety through the BOSH Electrical Safety Quiz Show.
  3. Develop students' technical expertise in digital drafting and electrical design through the EEllumination Design Contest.
  4. Strengthen practical competency in industrial automation, power systems, building wiring, and motor controls through the Skills Olympics.
  5. Encourage precision and technical competence in electrical installation while observing applicable Philippine Electrical Code provisions.
  6. Establish a platform for presenting academic research, technical innovations, and engineering developments.
  7. Familiarize students with industry trends, professional practices, and career opportunities through technical and non-technical learning activities.
